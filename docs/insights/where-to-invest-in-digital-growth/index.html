Digital strategy

Where should an established business invest in digital growth?

A useful roadmap begins with business priorities and the gap in the customer journey.

In this perspective

Name the commercial priorityFind the largest gapRespect dependenciesChoose a focused start when possibleReview before expanding
01

Name the commercial priority

Be specific about the change you want: more qualified inquiries, stronger local visibility, a clearer website or better coordination across channels.

02

Find the largest gap

Review how customers find you, evaluate your business and take the next step. The first investment should respond to the most important constraint.

03

Respect dependencies

A campaign needs a credible destination. Search content needs a useful site structure. Measurement needs a clear definition of success. Sequence the work accordingly.

04

Choose a focused start when possible

A defined project can resolve a clear issue. Ongoing content, visibility and optimization work may need a sustained partnership.

05

Review before expanding

Use the evidence from the first stage to decide what comes next. A roadmap should help you make choices, not commit you to activity without a rationale.
