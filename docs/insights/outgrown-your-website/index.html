7 signs your business has outgrown its website

A practical checklist for the gap between your business today and how it appears online.

In this perspective

Your services have changedYour best work is difficult to findYour positioning feels genericVisitors struggle to take the next stepMobile visitors get a weaker experienceYour visibility depends on your nameYou cannot connect activity to opportunity
01

Your services have changed

Your website still describes the business you were a few years ago. Review whether it explains your current services, ideal customers and reasons to choose you.

02

Your best work is difficult to find

Useful proof, expertise and project examples are hidden or outdated. Make the evidence customers need easy to evaluate.

03

Your positioning feels generic

The copy could describe almost any competitor. Explain what your business does, who it serves and why its approach is relevant.

04

Visitors struggle to take the next step

Contact paths are inconsistent or unclear. Walk through the journey from a service page to a qualified inquiry.

05

Mobile visitors get a weaker experience

Essential information or controls become difficult to use on smaller screens. Review the actual tasks customers need to complete.

06

Your visibility depends on your name

Existing customers can find you, but people searching for your services struggle to discover relevant pages. Connect service content with customer questions.

07

You cannot connect activity to opportunity

Traffic reports do not tell you whether the website creates useful inquiries. Define meaningful outcomes before investing in more activity.
