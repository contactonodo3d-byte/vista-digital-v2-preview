Measurement

Website traffic vs. qualified leads: what actually matters?

More visits are useful only when they support the opportunities your business wants.

In this perspective

Start with the business outcomeUnderstand the steps before the inquirySeparate volume from fitConnect channels to the websiteUse the numbers to ask better questions
01

Start with the business outcome

A law firm, dental practice and professional services business may have very different definitions of a valuable inquiry. Decide what makes an opportunity relevant before choosing digital measures.

02

Understand the steps before the inquiry

Consider how visitors arrive, which pages they use to evaluate your offer and where they decide to contact you. These steps give context to the final inquiry.

03

Separate volume from fit

An increase in traffic can contain people outside your service area or looking for something you do not offer. Review relevance alongside reach.

04

Connect channels to the website

Search, social and campaigns should lead to pages that answer the right questions and make the next step clear. A disconnect can create activity without qualified intent.

05

Use the numbers to ask better questions

If relevant visitors arrive but few inquire, review clarity, trust and friction. If the website converts useful visits but has little reach, visibility may deserve more attention.
