07 / Growth perspectives

Questions worth
asking first.

A few ways to think about the gap between business maturity and digital performance.

Is the website really the whole problem?+
A website can be one part of a larger gap. Look at whether the right audience finds it, whether it reflects the quality of your business, and whether visitors have a clear path to become customers. Improving all three together creates a more coherent growth system.
What should we measure?+
Start with business goals. Qualified inquiries, consultation requests and booked appointments are often more useful than traffic alone. Define what “qualified” means for your business and track the steps that lead to it.
Where should we start?+
Start by identifying the largest gap between your business priorities and your digital presence. Assess visibility, trust and conversion, then sequence the work around dependencies and likely business value.
